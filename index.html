Validate, test and optimise MCx and railway networks

Drone-mounted measurement — the Phoenix and Horus platforms — flies the routes and sites that matter for mission-critical communications and railway telecom, capturing RF conditions ground-based testing misses. The same flight programme extends to satellite-to-device coverage.

Phoenix drone platform→ Horus measurement device→ Satellite-to-device testing→

A secure home for everything collected

Every capture lands in a portal built for regulatory compliance and data security from the ground up — encrypted in transit and at rest, access-controlled by role, and structured for audit from day one.

Encrypted storage→ Role-based access→ Audit-ready logging→

From raw capture to tuning guidance

Collected data is analysed to produce concrete optimisation and tuning guidelines — not just a report of what's wrong, but a specific set of actions for network engineers to apply immediately.

AI-assisted analysis→ Optimisation guidelines→

A catalog of rApps, open and closed loop

Analysis becomes action through an rApp catalog built on the AI rApp Intent Studio platform — open-loop rApps for advisory tuning, closed-loop rApps that observe, analyse, decide and act automatically, matched to the use case.

Open-loop rApps→ Closed-loop rApps→ O-RAN alignment→
